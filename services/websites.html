What you get

A site that loads fast, ranks for your local search terms, and actually converts visitors into leads:

  • Custom design — no Wix, Squarespace, or template kits
  • Hand-written HTML/CSS/JS, optimized for Core Web Vitals
  • Mobile-first layout with thoughtful breakpoints
  • On-page SEO: structured data, meta, sitemap, alt text
  • Contact form wired to your email with spam protection
  • Lightweight analytics (Plausible-style — privacy-first)
  • SSL, fast hosting setup, custom domain configuration
  • Editing access plus a Loom walkthrough so you can update copy

Project sizes

  • Starter — from $500. Up to 5 pages. Home, About, Services, Reviews, Contact. Perfect for new service businesses on a tight budget.
  • Standard — from $1,500. Up to 12 pages, blog or news section, more advanced animations and interactions.
  • Custom — from $3,000. Booking flows, gated content, multi-language, custom CMS, light e-commerce, integrations.
  • Redesign of an existing site. Quoted based on what's there — usually 60–80% of a fresh build.

What we build

Most "AI" engagements are smarter automation with a sprinkle of LLM where it actually helps. Common projects:

  • Document and invoice processing — extract structured data from PDFs and emails
  • Customer-support assistants that draft replies from your past tickets
  • Lead intake and qualification flows wired into your CRM
  • Internal knowledge-base search across docs, drives, and email
  • Meeting transcription with structured action-item summaries
  • Content drafting tools tuned to your brand voice
  • Workflow automation across Zapier / n8n / Make / custom code
  • API integrations between tools that don't talk to each other

How we approach it

Audit first, build second. Most businesses don't need fancy AI — they need 2 or 3 sharp automations that close the gaps eating their team's time.

  • Workflow audit. Half-day on-site or remote. We map where time disappears.
  • Pick the smallest useful piece. Build one thing, ship it, measure the time saved.
  • Train your team. No tool survives if only one person knows how to run it.
  • Document & hand off. You should never feel locked into me as a vendor.

Heads-up: we won't sell you on AI when the answer is a better spreadsheet. Honest assessment first.

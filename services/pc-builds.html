What's included

Every build covers the full path from spec to a stress-tested machine on your desk:

  • Parts spec'd to your actual workload — gaming, work, creator, server
  • Transparent parts list at real retail prices (no markup, no kickbacks)
  • Full assembly with proper cable management
  • BIOS update, RAM training, SSD trim verification
  • Multi-hour CPU and GPU stress-test before handoff
  • Windows 11 (or your OS of choice) clean install with drivers
  • Optional: data migration from your old machine
  • Build-spec PDF and BIOS notes so future-you can upgrade easily

Build types

  • Office & everyday — $700–1,200. Quiet, fast for browsers, video calls, light work.
  • Gaming — 1080p/1440p — $1,200–2,000. Modern AAA at high settings, room to grow.
  • Creator workstation — $1,800–3,500. Video edit, 3D, music production, big RAM.
  • Flagship / 4K gaming — $3,000+. RTX 50-class, plenty of NVMe, premium thermals.
  • Existing-rig upgrades. Drop-in CPU/GPU/RAM/storage upgrades without a full rebuild.

Bringing your own parts? Totally fine — we'll just charge the build fee and flag anything we'd swap.

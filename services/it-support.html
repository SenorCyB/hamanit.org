What's covered

If it has a power button or an IP address, there's a good chance we work on it. Common jobs:

  • Slow computer diagnosis and tune-up
  • Malware, ransomware, and adware removal
  • Operating system reinstall and migration (Windows, macOS, Linux)
  • Hardware swap-outs — drives, RAM, fans, screens, batteries
  • Network and Wi-Fi troubleshooting, mesh setup, printer rescue
  • Email, account lockout, password reset chaos
  • Backup setup so you stop losing files
  • New computer setup, data migration, software install

Who it's for

Individuals, families, and small businesses in Greater Houston who want their tech to just work — without paying enterprise rates or fighting through a help desk script.

  • Home users with a stubborn laptop or family PC
  • Small offices with 1-15 machines and no in-house IT
  • Realtors, contractors, and solo professionals who can't afford downtime
  • Anyone burned by a big-box chain-shop experience

If your situation is bigger or weirder than usual, just ask — most "no one would touch this" problems turn out to be solvable.
